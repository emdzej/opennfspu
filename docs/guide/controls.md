# Controls

The game reads the keyboard directly, with the original's default keys: the arrow keys drive, and the
other controls (handbrake, gears, cameras, look behind, horn, pause) have keys of their own. The defaults
follow the keyboard's language, as in the original. The front end's menus use the mouse, as the original's
do.

**Options › Controls** shows every control's key, pad button, axis or hat and lets you change it. Click a
control, then press the new key or button. You can also reset the chosen controller to the original's
defaults, set the dead zones and choose each race camera's view. Your bindings are kept with your settings
(in the browser, in its storage for this site).

Gamepads are gasm's virtual pads (up to four, in the order they connect). The default for a pad is the
original's: the stick steers, up on it accelerates, down brakes, and the buttons give the handbrake, the
reset, the camera, looking behind and the gears. gasm has no force feedback, so its levels are kept but
nothing shakes.

gasm's own keys work in every runner: in `gasm-run`'s window, <kbd>F11</kbd> (or <kbd>Alt</kbd> +
<kbd>Enter</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> on macOS) switches fullscreen, and
holding <kbd>Esc</kbd> for a second quits. A short press of <kbd>Esc</kbd> is the game's: in a race it
opens the pause menu.
